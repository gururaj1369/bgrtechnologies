Privacy Commitment: This policy details how BGR Technologies ([Legal Entity: BGR Technologies Private Limited]) processes information gathered through our website https://www.bgrtechnologies.in. We do not sell your personal data or transmit it to unauthorized third-party brokers.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy outlines how BGR Technologies ("we," "our," or "us") collects, utilizes, stores, and protects personal information submitted by visitors and commercial prospects ("you" or "user"). We operate in accordance with applicable data protection regulations, including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (India) and international privacy principles such as the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) for international inquiries.

2. Information Collected Through the Website

When you browse our Website, our servers automatically log standard network telemetry to maintain site stability and security. This may include:

  • Your Internet Protocol (IP) address.
  • Browser type, version, and operating system.
  • Referring URL and pages visited on our site.
  • Date, timestamp, and duration of visit.

This automated data is used solely for technical diagnostics, bot defense, and server health monitoring.

3. Information Submitted Voluntarily Via Email & Direct Inquiries

When you contact us via email at info@bgrtechnologies.in or telephone, you voluntarily provide information such as:

  • Full Name & Title: To address you and create client communication records.
  • Business Email Address: To respond to your inquiry and provide proposals.
  • Phone Number (Optional): To facilitate telephone consultation if requested.
  • Company / Organization Name: To understand your business context and operational scale.
  • Project Specifications: Details you provide regarding your software, Generative Engine Optimization, or marketing requirements.

4. Purposes of Processing Personal Information

We process your personal information strictly for legitimate business purposes:

  • To evaluate your project requirements and deliver customized proposals.
  • To communicate directly with you regarding inquiries and scheduled consultations.
  • To establish and execute contractual agreements for approved services.
  • To protect the website against malicious automated bots and denial-of-service attempts.
  • To comply with statutory financial and legal documentation obligations under Indian law.

5. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Our website is engineered as a clean, lightweight static application. We do not employ tracking cookies, invasive third-party cross-site trackers, or advertising pixels on this website.

6. Analytics and Website Performance Measurement

We may monitor aggregated, non-personally identifiable metrics (such as page response speeds, device categories, and error rates) to improve user experience. If analytics tools are active, IP anonymization is enforced to prevent individual tracking.

7. Marketing Communications and Consent

We do not add your contact information to unsolicited marketing newsletters or promotional blast lists. We will contact you exclusively regarding your specific project inquiry and related commercial discussions unless you explicitly request ongoing industry updates.

8. Third-Party Service Providers

We engage trusted third-party infrastructure providers who process data strictly under contractual confidentiality, including:

  • Hosting & Server Infrastructure: Cloud hosting facilities with physical access controls, firewall protection, and SSL encryption.
  • Business Email Services: Enterprise cloud email providers used to receive and respond to inquiries.

These service providers are prohibited from using your data for any independent marketing or secondary purpose.

9. Data Storage and Retention

Direct correspondence records are retained securely in encrypted business accounts for as long as necessary to fulfill the communication lifecycle, maintain client history, and satisfy statutory tax and business audit periods (typically up to three (3) years), after which data is securely purged.

10. Information Security Practices

We implement administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect personal information:

  • Enforced HTTPS transport layer security (TLS/SSL encryption) on all website connections.
  • Search-engine protection and runtime obfuscation of direct communication handles.
  • Restricted access controls ensuring only authorized project leads review correspondence.

11. Data Sharing and Disclosures

BGR Technologies does not sell, rent, lease, or monetize your personal data. We disclose personal information only under the following limited circumstances:

  • With your explicit prior authorization.
  • To comply with valid legal processes, court orders, or enforceable governmental requests under Indian law.
  • To enforce our Terms and Conditions or protect the safety and property of BGR Technologies, our users, or the public.

12. International Data Transfers

If you access our Website from outside India, your information may be processed and stored in India or in server locations operated by our hosting partners. We ensure that international transfers comply with applicable data protection laws through standard contractual clauses and rigorous technical protections.

13. User Rights and Applicable Privacy Choices

Subject to applicable laws (including India's DPDP Act, 2023 and GDPR), you have specific rights regarding your personal information:

  • Right to Access: Request confirmation of whether we process your data and receive a copy.
  • Right to Correction: Request prompt correction of inaccurate or incomplete personal records.
  • Right to Erasure: Request the deletion of your correspondence records when no longer required for legitimate business or legal purposes.
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: Revoke consent for direct correspondence at any time.

To exercise these rights, please email our privacy desk at info@bgrtechnologies.in.

14. Children's Privacy

Our Website and commercial services are strictly intended for business enterprises and individuals over the age of eighteen (18). We do not knowingly solicit or collect personal information from minors. If you believe a minor has submitted data to us, please notify us immediately for prompt deletion.

15. Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect operational or regulatory changes. When updates occur, the revision date at the top of this document will be updated. We encourage periodic review of this page.
